Frequently asked questions

How quickly can you get to a broken boiler?

Most repairs in Stoke-on-Trent and North Staffordshire are attended same day or next day. Send an enquiry and we'll tell you straight when we can come.

Are you Gas Safe registered?

Yes. All gas work, repairs, servicing and certificates, is carried out by Gas Safe registered engineers, and we're happy to show our registration details.

Do you give a price before starting work?

Always. For repairs we diagnose first, explain what's wrong, and quote before any work begins. Bathroom installations are quoted in full before we touch anything.

Can you handle landlord properties with tenants in?

Yes. We arrange access times with tenants directly, and landlords get all certificates and service records sent promptly.

How long does a bathroom installation take?

A standard full refit usually runs one to two weeks depending on tiling and parts. We give you a realistic schedule at the quote stage and stick to it.

What areas do you cover?

Stoke-on-Trent and the wider North Staffordshire area. If you're just outside, send an enquiry and we'll confirm whether we can reach you.
